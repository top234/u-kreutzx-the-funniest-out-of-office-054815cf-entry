# Call the Fire Brigade

A single-line out-of-office auto-reply, written to be the funniest one in the inbox: the sender is away until Monday, and if your email is on fire, you should call the fire brigade — they answer faster than the sender does on a normal Tuesday.

> Out of office until Monday. If your email is on fire, call the fire brigade — they answer faster than I do on a normal Tuesday.

(127 characters, under the 140-character limit.)

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the one-line reply to `${OUTPUT_DIR:-/output}/entry.txt` (no trailing newline).
