# Number 47 in the Queue

This out-of-office reply does not promise a reply at all — it gives your email a number instead. You are number 47 in a queue that will be deleted on my return, lovingly, one by one, without ever being read. The politeness is real, the queue is real, and the reading is not: every sender gets the same honest, numbered fate, and the only thing waiting for them on Monday is the delete key, pressed with love.

> Out of office until Monday. Your email is number 47 in a queue I will delete on my return, lovingly, one by one, without reading.
